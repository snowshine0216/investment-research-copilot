# Skipped items — rationale and unblock path

These three items from the analysis are not in this loop. They need a human design decision, new data ingestion, or both.

## B10 — Rewrite synthesizer for per-claim citations

**What it asks.** Replace the current synthesizer (`src/irc/memo/synthesizer.py`) with a two-step flow where the LLM emits bullet-citation pairs and a post-processor flags uncited claims as `[未引用]`. Move the traceability metric from "verbatim substring hits in the appendix" (currently a guaranteed pass) to "fraction of bulleted claims with a `[ref_id]`".

**Why blocked.**
- The synthesizer prompt is the load-bearing piece of the entire memo pipeline. A redesign changes the report's voice and adds variance to every downstream audit. Worth a design pass that includes prompt eval runs, not an autodev sprint.
- The shape of `MemoOutput.traceability` is part of the decision-gate contract (`src/irc/decision/gates.py:memo_evidence_status`). Changing its semantics ripples into `decide_row` and the JSON schema.
- Item 006 (numeric-prose sanity validator) covers the most user-visible failure mode (the `cheap`/`valuation_cost=85` contradiction) without rewriting the prompt. Most value, smallest blast radius.

**Unblock.** Design doc + prompt-eval harness comparing the current single-pass synthesizer against a citation-first variant on the existing 2026-05-17 and 2026-05-18 evidence pools. Decide on a winner, then port `decide_row` together.

## C12 — Zero-weight portfolio holdings the account cannot execute

**What it asks.** When an instrument has `venue_compatible=false` and no proxy, the allocation should remove its weight and redistribute (rather than today's behavior of holding ~20% of the portfolio in two gold ETFs the user's account cannot trade). Today these positions appear in `proposed_allocation.yaml` with their full weight but are flagged in `trade_plan.yaml` as `venue_compatible: false`.

**Why blocked.**
- This is a portfolio policy change, not a bug. It affects every report the user receives going forward, and may surprise users who have existing positions they cannot trade today but want to keep in the model.
- Item 010 (allow paper-gold as a same-class proxy) addresses the specific gold case directly. After it ships, the venue-blocked weight on the gold positions drops to zero on its own.
- The right shape for this is probably configurable: a preferences-level `redistribute_when_blocked: true|false`. That belongs in `inputs/preferences.yaml`, not in code defaults.

**Unblock.** Add the preferences flag (default `false` to preserve today's behavior), wire it into `irc/allocation/target_weights.py`, and document the trade-off in the README.

## C14 — Wire a real macro evidence block

**What it asks.** Today `memo.md` Section 2 ("宏观环境") is generated by the LLM extrapolating from per-instrument valuation scores. There is no macro-side evidence (real yields, DXY, CB net buying, A-share PE percentile, US 10Y) in the prompt. Result: claims like "黄金主线 intact" with no quoted macro datum.

**Why blocked.**
- Requires new data ingestion. `config/macro_view.yaml` exists as a placeholder; there is no source connector for real-yield (TIPS), DXY, China CB gold holdings, or A-share aggregate PE.
- akshare has partial coverage (e.g. `bond_zh_us_rate` for US Treasury yields, `currency_boc_safe` for FX), but no single drop-in source for the full macro panel. The right shape is probably 3-4 small ingest modules + a `macro_snapshot` artifact that the memo pipeline reads.

**Unblock.** A separate data-ingest backlog: pick 4 macro inputs (real_yield_10y_tips, dxy, sp500_pe_pct, china_10y), connect each, validate freshness, then thread `macro_snapshot` into `MemoInputs` and the synthesizer prompt. This is on the order of an evening, not 30 minutes — out of scope for this loop.
